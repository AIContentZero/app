1. Introduction

VIDZON ("we", "our", "us") is an AI-powered application for generating images and videos from your text prompts and photos. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we request your name and email address from Apple. Your account is managed through Google Firebase Authentication. We process an Apple identity token and a Firebase ID token to authenticate you.
  • Content you provide. The text prompts you write and the photos you upload (including reference photos used for character-based video generation) are transmitted to our servers and to third-party AI providers in order to generate your images and videos.
  • Transaction and usage data. We store your wallet balance, subscription status, and generation history so the app can display them to you.
  • Photo library access. With your permission, the app saves generated images and videos to your photo library.
  • Purchase data. Subscriptions and coin purchases are billed by Apple through the App Store. We receive transaction verification data from Apple to activate your subscription or credit your account.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not run analytics in the app (Firebase Analytics is disabled).
  • We do not serve advertisements or use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • The app does not request access to your location.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the generation service (processing prompts and photos into images and videos).
  • To authenticate you and manage your account.
  • To process payments, subscriptions, and your wallet balance.
  • To save generated media to your photo library when you permit it.
  • To respond to support requests.

5. How We Share Your Information

  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple and App Store billing.
  • Google Firebase — for authentication.
  • Third-party AI providers — your prompts and uploaded photos are transmitted to third-party AI generation providers to generate your images and videos.
  • Hosting and service providers — our backend infrastructure stores and processes this data to operate the service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the service. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your account and anonymizes associated personal data on our servers.

7. Your Privacy Choices

  • Delete your account. You can delete your account and anonymize associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account in the app.
  • Access or delete your data. You can contact us at tatyanadotsenko93@gmail.com to request access to or deletion of your personal information.
  • No sale or advertising use. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, and API requests are authenticated with token-based credentials. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date above and make the revised policy available in the app and on this page.
